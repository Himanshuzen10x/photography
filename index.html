About The Studio

Where Light Meets Emotion

Founded in 2018, Eternal Frames Studio began with a single camera and a simple belief — that wedding photography should feel like memory, not performance. Today, our team of photographers, cinematographers and editors has documented hundreds of weddings and events across Kanpur and beyond, chasing light, emotion and the small unscripted moments that make every celebration unforgettable.
